Privacy policy

LMES Portal runs only on my own computer. It connects to my Google Calendar with read-only access, so it can match each lesson to a student using event titles and times.

This information is not sent to any server and is not shared with anyone else. The sign-in details and lesson history are stored only in files on my computer.

I can disconnect at any time by removing the app's access in my Google Account under Third-party apps, and by deleting the portal's calendar files from my lessons folder.
